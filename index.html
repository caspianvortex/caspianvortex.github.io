About

Caspian Vortex is a rep volleyball team from Newmarket, Ontario, part of Caspian Sports Club. The team started at 12U in 2024-25, played 13U in 2025-26, and is playing 14U this season. It travels to tournaments across the season and goes to the Ontario provincials and national championships.

Season by season

  1. 2026-27, 14U

    Current season. The goal is the Ontario provincials and the 14U national championship.

  2. 2025-26, 13U

    10 gold and 2 silver medals. 4th at the Ontario provincials and 15th at the 14U national championship.

  3. 2024-25, 12U

    8 gold and 2 silver medals. 1st and 2nd place at the Ontario provincials.
